Our Story

Skeleton Collective presents our custom made 3D desk trinkets. A passion project we've been working on to help generate funds for our projects.

A corresponding character for film set roles, we hope these cute lil guys find their home on your desk! If you have an idea for the next role you would like to see represented, please leave a vote in the comment section down below.

Generating original narrative content is extremely resource intensive. We are currently putting our Time/Money/Soul on the line in hopes that one day we will be your flagship studio. And we will.

Buying these products will help us mitigate initial loss, as well as expanding our brand!

Skel.Col is our merch and Short Form Brand, which will help push us along the next couple years as we prep larger projects for Skeleton Collective theatrical releases.
